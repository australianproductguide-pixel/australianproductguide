# APG SEO Authority & Social Distribution — v58

Status: **CURRENT OPERATING GUARDRAIL**  
Effective: 22 August 2026

## Objective

Grow Australian Product Guide through genuinely useful Australian product research and distribution, not profile-count inflation, repetitive affiliate promotion or manufactured links.

The governing question remains: **What should I actually buy for my situation, and why?**

## Earned-authority priority

APG should prioritise material that credible third parties would have a reason to reference independently:

1. Australian shopping datasets and decision studies with transparent methodology.
2. Category research that explains meaningful consumer trade-offs rather than repackaging specifications.
3. Source-linked changes in product ranges, standards, retailer availability or buying conditions.
4. Reusable charts/tables where the underlying dataset, scope, date and limitations are visible.
5. Manufacturer, retailer, industry and journalist outreach only when the underlying APG material is genuinely relevant to their audience.

### Link governance

- Never buy links or exchange links for ranking benefit.
- Never mass-submit APG to low-quality directories solely for backlinks.
- Never manufacture third-party reviews, endorsements, partnerships or citations.
- Never claim hands-on testing unless it is explicitly documented.
- Affiliate economics contribute zero recommendation points.
- Prefer fewer credible references over a large volume of weak profiles.

## Social distribution roles

All six verified APG channels should deep-link to the most relevant APG research page rather than defaulting to the homepage.

| Channel | Primary role | Best APG destinations | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pinterest | Evergreen visual shopping discovery | Category hubs, buying guides, decision explainers | Repetitive product-affiliate Pins |
| LinkedIn | Research standards and product intelligence | Methodology, sources, original studies, platform/research updates | Generic consumer deal spam |
| Instagram | Visual comparisons and consumer education | Comparisons, category guides, finders | Feed of outbound affiliate links |
| Facebook | Useful comparison and buying guidance | Guides, comparisons, category hubs | Reposting every Instagram item unchanged |
| X | Concise trade-offs and timely research observations | Focused comparisons, updates, research pages | Automated link-only posting |
| Threads | Conversational trade-offs and shopping questions | Comparisons, guides, Help Me Choose | Duplicating X word-for-word by default |

## Content pattern

A useful APG social post should normally do at least one of these:

- explain a meaningful trade-off;
- answer a specific buying question;
- visualise a category decision;
- surface a material research update;
- show what changed in a maintained dataset;
- help a shopper rule an option in or out.

The post should make sense without a click. The linked APG page should provide the deeper evidence, comparison or decision tool.

## Comparison-page governance

Pairwise comparisons are a curated SEO surface, not a Cartesian-product generator.

Current code enforces:

- maximum **14** indexable prepared comparisons per category;
- maximum **300** indexable prepared pair pages site-wide without an explicit governance change;
- distinct products and a decision distinction signal before a pair can be prepared.

Adding products must not automatically create every possible comparison URL. A future increase in the cap requires a deliberate review of search demand, page differentiation, evidence depth, crawl/indexation quality and consumer usefulness.

## Social imagery governance

- Category pages, category buying guides, finders and category comparison surfaces may use the same provenance-backed editorial category image already published by APG when source, licence, creator and review date are present.
- Category editorial imagery is contextual only and must not be represented as a reviewed product image.
- Product pages may use product-specific social imagery only when the image registry verifies rights basis, exact or immaterial-variant product identity, verification date and applicable retailer/Amazon requirements.
- Until those product-image gates pass, the generic APG social card remains safer than an unverified product photograph.

## Measurement

Assess distribution on outcomes that indicate useful discovery rather than vanity activity:

- qualified landing sessions by destination page;
- Search Console social/video-platform visibility where available;
- engaged visits into categories, products, comparisons and finders;
- saves/shares or platform engagement where observable;
- earned referring domains and citations;
- growth in non-branded organic discovery;
- indexation and search performance of substantive APG research.

No traffic, revenue, ranking, conversion or authority improvement is to be claimed without measured evidence.
